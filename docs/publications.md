# Publications & Academic Honors

A summary of peer-reviewed scientific contributions in biomedical engineering, electromyographic signal processing, digital systems, and awards.

---

## :material-trophy: Honors & Awards

- :material-medal: **RETECA Foundation Scholarship** *(Sept 2018)*  
  Awarded by the Reteca Foundation, granting selected international young engineers the opportunity to conduct applied research at SUPSI (*Switzerland*) and complete a postgraduate Master of Science in Engineering degree.

- :material-certificate: **Certificate of Merit** *(March 2018)*  
  Awarded for the paper *"Myoelectric Response of the Upper Limbs to Temperature Variation"* at the **2018 International Conference on Bioinformatics (IMECS 2018)**, Hong Kong.

- :material-certificate: **Certificate of Merit** *(July 2017)*  
  Awarded for the paper *"A Dual Extended Kalman Filter for Tilt Estimation"* at the **2017 International Conference of Signal and Image Engineering (WCE 2017)**, London, United Kingdom.

- :material-school: **Scholarship for Scientific Merit** *(May 2015)*  
  Recognition for Technical and Innovation Merit (I Term 2015), Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral (ESPOL).
